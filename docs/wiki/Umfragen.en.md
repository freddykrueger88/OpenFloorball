# 🗳️ Polls

*[🇩🇪 Deutsch](Umfragen.md) | 🇬🇧 English*

Under `/polls`, a coach or club official asks their team a short
question with several answer options ("Which day works best?", "New
jersey color?"). Team members vote, and results are visible to
everyone immediately – no waiting for a "reveal".

## Scope (deliberately kept simple)

Same "minimal" philosophy as [News](./News.en.md): **no commenting/
discussion** on a poll.

- Only a team's coach/owner may **create** polls. Team members vote
  (that's enough for read access, same as with RSVP).
- Every poll belongs to **exactly one team** – no personal use case, no
  team means no polls.
- **Single choice** (default): clicking an option replaces any previous
  vote of yours in that poll. Clicking your current selection again
  removes your vote.
- **Multiple choice** (enabled at creation): each option can be
  selected and deselected independently.
- **Closing** (coach/owner only) prevents further votes – handy for
  locking in a decision once enough people have voted. Votes already
  cast remain visible.
- Any coach/owner of the team may **delete** a poll, not just whoever
  created it.
- Questions/options can't be edited after creation – if there's a
  mistake, delete it and create a new one.
