# 🎯 Positions & Roles

*[🇩🇪 Deutsch](IFF-Positionen.md) | 🇬🇧 English*

OpenFloorball uses the standard large-field **2-1-2** system (2
defenders, 1 center, 2 attackers) – unlike soccer, there's no
"midfield" line; the middle field-player position in floorball is
called the **center**.

| Code | Position | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **TW** | Goalkeeper | Guards the goal, allowed to play the ball with any body part inside the goal crease. Organizes the defense, starts counterattacks with quick throw-outs. |
| **V** | Defender | Secures their own half, supports the goalkeeper, initiates attacks. |
| **C** | Center | Link between defense and offense, covers the most ground, offers passing options between the lines. |
| **S** | Attacker | Actively looks to finish on goal, presses the opposing defense, opens space for the center. |

These short hints (incl. tips) appear in the info panel as soon as a
player is selected in the board editor (can be enabled via "Position
hints" in the [Settings tab](./Einstellungen.en.md)).

## Count per field type

Only the large field has a goalkeeper – on the small field, street,
and 3v3, only field players play (see
[Field types & dimensions](./IFF-Spielfelder.en.md)).
