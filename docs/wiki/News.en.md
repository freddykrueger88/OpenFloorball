# 📣 News

*[🇩🇪 Deutsch](News.md) | 🇬🇧 English*

Under `/news`, a coach or club manager posts short announcements to
their team ("Training on Tuesday is cancelled", "Please wash jerseys
by Friday") – all team members see them in a chronological list,
newest first.

## Scope (deliberately kept simple)

Deliberately **not a full chat**: no comments, no replies, no
read-receipt status. A full chat would mean high moderation effort for
little added value, given teams already use WhatsApp & co. for the
rest of their communication anyway – News just fills the gap of "one
short, official announcement to everyone" more cheaply.

- **Posting** is limited to a team's coach/owner (like the match squad
  or setting RSVP deadlines – a coaching decision). Team members only
  read.
- Every announcement belongs to **exactly one team** – there's no
  personal announcement without a team (a team is the audience). If
  you're not on any team, there's nothing to see here.
- If you're coach/owner of multiple teams, you choose which team the
  announcement goes to when posting. The feed itself shows
  announcements from all your teams together, with a team name badge
  on each row.
- Any coach/owner of the respective team can delete – not just whoever
  posted the announcement themselves (e.g. if the head coach needs to
  correct an assistant coach's announcement). For a typo: delete and
  repost, there's no editing.
